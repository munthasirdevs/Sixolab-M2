Engagement Criteria: Working with Xenon Studios

Clear collaboration criteria for brand strategy, identity and digital product design.

CRITERIA // 01

Best Fit For

  • New businesses that need a strong identity from day one.
  • Established brands repositioning or modernizing visual touchpoints.
  • Product teams that need considered UI/UX and scalable design systems.
CRITERIA // 02

What We Need From You

  • A brief covering business goals, audience and competitors.
  • Visual references: what you love and what you refuse to look like.
  • Existing brand assets, vector logos and typography if any.
  • One primary approver and consolidated feedback per review round.
CRITERIA // 03

What You Receive

  • A strategy and creative direction document.
  • Concept routes presented with clear architectural rationale.
  • Final production files in every format required for multi-channel use.
  • Brand guidelines and design token tokens for consistent future use.
CRITERIA // 04

How We Engage

  • Discovery session to align on market positioning.
  • Concept presentation, followed by iterative development.
  • A defined number of revision rounds per phase.
  • Structured handover of all master Figma and vector assets.
