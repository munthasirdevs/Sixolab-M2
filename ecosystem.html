Engagement Criteria: Working with Sixolab Directly

Transparent requirements and deliverable standards for group-level engagements.

CRITERIA // 01

Best Fit For

  • Businesses that need more than one discipline at once.
  • Organizations launching a brand or product end to end.
  • Partners and investors exploring venture collaboration.
CRITERIA // 02

What We Need From You

  • A clear business objective and a named decision-maker.
  • Willingness to share context: goals, constraints, existing assets.
  • Timely feedback and approvals at agreed checkpoints.
CRITERIA // 03

What You Receive

  • One point of contact across every company involved.
  • A unified proposal and a coordinated timeline.
  • Strategic oversight from discovery through delivery.
CRITERIA // 04

How We Engage

  • Group-level discovery to define the full scope.
  • Work then scoped and assigned to the right company.
  • Sixolab coordinates and stays accountable throughout.
