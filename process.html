General Criteria: What Makes a Project Run Well Across Every Company

Five foundational rules that guarantee execution velocity and project success.

CRITERION // 01

A clear objective

"We build better when we know what success looks like." Whether launching a product or repositioning a brand, clarity of purpose drives speed and prevents scope drift.

CRITERION // 02

One decision-maker

"Faster approvals, fewer conflicting rounds." Having a single designated point of contact with sign-off authority ensures momentum is never lost to committee hesitation.

CRITERION // 03

Timely feedback

"Schedules hold when review happens when agreed." Engineering and film production schedules depend on predictable review turnarounds at predetermined sprint gates.

CRITERION // 04

Access to assets and people

"Brand files, systems, data and key stakeholders." Our specialists work as embedded extensions of your team, requiring direct access to context and tooling.

CRITERION // 05

Written scope

"Every engagement is documented before work begins, and changes are agreed in writing." Transparent documentation protects both budgets and timelines.

PAYMENT SCHEDULE OPTIONS
SCHEDULE // 01

50% Upfront, 50% Completion

Begin with 50% payment to start work, and pay the remaining 50% only after the final deliverable is reviewed and approved.

SCHEDULE // 02

Milestone Payments

Structured releases tied to reviewable project stages (e.g., 25% kick-off, 25% wireframes, 25% staging, 25% handover).

SCHEDULE // 03

Secure Escrow Payment

Funds are held safely by a trusted third-party escrow and released only after you verify and approve the completed deliverables.

SCHEDULE // 04

Fixed Monthly Retainer

Pay a fixed amount each month on a specific due date, providing predictable operational expenditure.

SCHEDULE // 05

Monthly 50/50 Split

50% within the first 10 days of the month, and 50% within the last 10 days—balancing cash flow and continuous commitment.

SCHEDULE // 06

Tailored Payment Schedule

We'll design a completely custom payment plan together that aligns perfectly with your business cash flow and project milestones.
